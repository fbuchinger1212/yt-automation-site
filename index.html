yt-automation

A private upload tool for my own YouTube channels

yt-automation is a personal tool run by Felix Buchinger on his own computer. It edits short videos (YouTube Shorts) and uploads them to YouTube channels that he owns and manages.

What the app does

  • Prepares short vertical videos with captions on the owner's computer.
  • Uploads approved videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and schedules their publication time, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads the basic channel information (name and profile picture) of the connected channels.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in with it, and only to channels he controls.

Google sign-in

The app asks for two permissions: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and viewing the connected channel (youtube.readonly). Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
